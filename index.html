FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions

How does DuoReveal detect my MacBook hinge angle?

DuoReveal connects to the Apple Sensor Processing Unit (SPU) over the internal IOKit HID bus (HIDReport 7). This provides 0.01° sub-degree angular precision in real time without optical cameras or battery drain.

Does DuoReveal drain battery while I work?

No. When your MacBook is open normally (≥ 90°), screen capture is 100% idle (0.0% CPU and 0.0% GPU). The Metal render loop and ScreenCaptureKit only engage the moment your lid is folded down.

Why does DuoReveal ask for Screen Recording permission?

macOS requires Screen Recording permission for any app that reads on-screen window pixels. DuoReveal uses Apple's official ScreenCaptureKit framework to capture an instantaneous single-frame snapshot into GPU memory when you close the lid.
